**Ember Duel** — an original card duel at a moth lantern festival. Play Lanterns to burn your rival's Embers, Moths to guard, Wind to draw, Wax to mend. Read the rival's telegraphed intent, counter it, and do not let your own Embers die.

## Play

Open `game/index.html` in a desktop browser. Click a card or press **1–5** to play it. Press **Enter** or click **End turn** to resolve the exchange. Open **How to play** at the table for the suit and combo rules.

## Package the entry

With Node 22:

```sh
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out node main.ts
```
